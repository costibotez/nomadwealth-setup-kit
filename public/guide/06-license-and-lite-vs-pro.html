Setup Kit / Lite vs Pro

06 / Choose your depth

Lite is a real install. Pro removes the caps.

Both tiers run the same self-hosted application on your Vercel and Neon. Lite is free forever; Pro is a one-time license activated offline.

Lite · €0

Track the daily loop

Core tracking with no card, no key, and no time limit.

  • Up to 100 transactions
  • 3 browser-push alerts
  • 5 watchlist tickers
  • 2 display currencies
  • 1 property and 1 private loan
  • Core overview, holdings, import, properties, loans and watchlist

Pro · one-time license

Own the full cockpit

All caps removed, plus the deeper analysis and reporting tabs.

  • Unlimited transactions, alerts, watchlist and currencies
  • Email alerts alongside browser push
  • Performance, dividends and FIRE projection
  • Tax pack, data health and read-only sharing
  • Businesses, clients and read-only Connect Claude MCP
  • Updates are free for life

What a cap does

Caps apply when creating new rows. Existing data remains visible, editable, deletable, and exportable. If a Lite install is already above a cap after an update, the app does not hide or delete anything; it blocks only the next insert and explains the Pro path.

Activation is offline

The Pro key is checked against an Ed25519 public key embedded in the app. The optional revocation check is opaque and sends only the key. A network failure does not brick the install. You can also finish setup without a key and activate later from the License tab.

Privacy is not a tier feature. Lite and Pro both keep the database in your Neon and the application in your Vercel. The vendor does not receive your net-worth data.
