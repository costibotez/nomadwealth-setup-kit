07 / Import

Preview first. Commit deliberately.

NomadWealth parses spreadsheet files in your browser, maps recognizable columns, and lets you inspect the result before anything is committed to your Neon database.

Privacy boundary: selecting a file for preview does not upload it to the vendor or this kit. The app’s browser parser reads the first sheet locally.

Supported files and limits

  • CSV, XLSX, and XLS files are supported by the web importer.
  • Files over 15 MB are refused before parsing. Split a large export at the source instead of weakening the limit.
  • Only the first worksheet is previewed by the browser parser.
  • Inspect the file yourself first and remove unrelated sheets or columns where appropriate.

Import by asset class

After setup, use the importer and choose the destination that matches the record: public holdings and transactions, property, private loans, cash, or business records. Do not force a property or loan export into a stock transaction template. If a source export cannot be represented faithfully, keep the original file and enter the exceptional rows manually.

Safe workflow

  1. Keep the original export

    Work from a copy and preserve the untouched source in your own encrypted storage.

  2. Map and review

    Confirm dates, direction, currencies, quantities, and cost fields. Unrecognised columns are shown as ignored; do not assume they are harmless until you review them.

  3. Commit in small batches

    Start with a small, representative set. Check the resulting rows in the cockpit before importing the rest.
